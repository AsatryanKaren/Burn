## Your place: how and when it is delivered

[Light up Armenia](/en/map/) is a digital night map of Armenia with 111,111 plots of 500×500 m each. There are only this many places this year, and the map will not grow.

This is a symbolic digital marker: you do not acquire any land, property or right to a real place. The borders on the map are approximate and illustrative only (see section 7 of the [Terms of Service](/en/terms/)).

1. **While you pay**, the plot you chose is reserved for you for 30 minutes.
2. **Right after payment** your place appears on the map and you are taken to your personal page, with a link to your place. If you left an email, we also send you the link.
3. **Your room** is yours to decorate. You can change it later at any time.
4. **Any visitor** can tap your place, step into your room and send you warmth, and see its message, its signature (if approved), the date, the province and the nearest town. Your email is never shown.

**What counts as delivered:** your place has appeared on the map and your personal page is available.

**If your plot is taken:** each plot can belong to only one person. If, rarely, someone else takes it at the same moment, we offer you the nearest free plot or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

**How long your place stays on the map:** at least until 31 January 2027. After that, we may keep the map as a read-only archive page or remove it.

**If your message or signature is hidden** while it is checked or because it broke the rules, your place still stays on the map, just without the visible message or signature.

## Your wish: how and when it is delivered

1. **You write your wish** right after buying your place, or later, from the "Fire" tab on the [map](/en/map/). It is up to 120 characters: something you want to leave behind in 2026.
2. **Within minutes** of writing it, if your place is paid, your wish appears in the fire on the website. On your personal page you then see its number, a replay of it burning and your story image.
3. **From 20 to 31 December 2026** the fire is broadcast live on YouTube. Wishes appear on the stream one after another. Wishes written before 20 December burn on the website right away and join the stream queue when it starts.

**What counts as delivered:** your wish has burned in the fire on the website and your personal page is available. The YouTube stream is an extra way to watch: if very many wishes are written in the last hours of 31 December, or if YouTube is interrupted, we cannot guarantee that every wish is shown on the stream, but every wish of a paid place burns on the website and in its replay.

**How long your page is available:** at least until 31 January 2027.

**If your text is hidden by moderation** (for example, it contains a real person's name, a phone number or a link), your wish still burns, but its text is not shown publicly. You still see it on your personal page.

## Before payments open

If card payments are not open yet when you visit, you can save your place with your email. You are **not charged** anything. The plot is not reserved then: if it is taken before payments open, we offer you the nearest free plot. When payments open, we email you a link to pay.

You can already write your wish for a saved place. It goes into the fire as soon as your place is paid.

## How payment works

1. You press the payment button on the order page.
2. You are taken to the **bank's own secure payment page**. You enter your card details there, not on our site. We never see or store your card number or CVV.
3. Your bank may ask you to confirm the payment with a one-time code (3-D Secure).
4. After the bank confirms the payment to us directly, you are returned to your personal page.

If the payment fails, no money is taken and your order stays unpaid. You can try again.

## Currency and foreign cards

All payments are made in **Armenian drams (AMD)**. Cards issued abroad are charged in AMD; your card issuer converts the amount to your card's currency at its own rate and may charge a fee.

Cards issued in Russia generally do not work with Armenian banks. If you are in Russia, a relative or friend in Armenia can pay for you using the link to your order.

## Payment confirmation and receipt

After payment you see a confirmation on your personal page and, if you left an email, receive it by email. Your bank also shows the payment in your card statement.
