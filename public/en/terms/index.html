2. What the service is

New Year Fire is a digital New Year ritual. You write something you want to leave behind in the old year (up to 120 characters), pay for it, and your paper burns in a shared digital fire on the website and on our YouTube live stream, which runs from 20 to 31 December 2026.

The fire is a computer animation. No physical paper is burned and nothing physical is sent to you.

After payment you receive:

  • a personal page with your paper's number and a replay of your paper burning;
  • a story image you can download and share.

6. What you may write

Your paper is shown in a public fire and on a public live stream, so some rules apply. Do not write:

  • names or surnames of real people (except the first name of the recipient in the "For a friend" field);
  • insults, threats, hate speech or anything humiliating people because of their nationality, religion, gender or any other trait;
  • phone numbers, addresses, links, usernames or other contact details;
  • advertising;
  • anything illegal under the laws of the Republic of Armenia.

How we handle content:

  • Before payment, our system may refuse a text that clearly breaks these rules. You can rewrite it; you are not charged.
  • Texts that may contain names, phone numbers or links can be accepted but hidden. A hidden paper still burns and counts as delivered, but its text is never shown publicly. You still see it on your own personal page.
  • Every text is checked before it can appear publicly. Until it is checked, your paper burns without visible text.
  • You can choose when ordering not to show your text publicly at all.
  • We may hide any text at any time if we believe it breaks these rules.
  • If, after payment, we have to refuse a text completely because it is illegal, the paper does not burn and we refund the payment in full.

Permission to show your text. By ordering, you allow us to show your text (if it is approved and you chose to show it) in the fire on the website, on the live stream and in the stream recordings, free of charge, for the purposes of this service. If you later want your text removed from public view, write to us; see the Privacy Policy.

You are responsible for what you write. Do not write anything you would not want strangers to read.

7. The 2027 wish

With "Burn + 2027 wish" and "Midnight fire" you also write a wish for next year (up to 200 characters).

  • Your wish is never shown publicly before it is opened.
  • On 31 December 2027 we hold a live stream where the wishes are "opened". Each wish is shown to its author on their personal page. The live stream shows the moment of opening, not the private text of your wish.
  • If you left an email, we will send you a reminder before the opening.
  • After the opening, the wish stays on your personal page for 30 days and is then deleted.

8. For a friend

  • You write the paper on behalf of another person and add only their first name (up to 40 characters; no surname).
  • You get a personal link to send them. Anyone with this link can see the paper and its text, so share it only with that person.
  • Only use a first name of someone you know and who, as you reasonably believe, would not mind.

11. The 20% for children's gifts

From every payment we set aside 20% of the amount paid (rounded down to whole drams) to buy New Year gifts for children in Armenian orphanages.

  • The gifts are bought and delivered in January 2027 (between 6 and 13 January). We will publish receipts and a report on the website.
  • If a payment is refunded, its 20% share is removed from the gift fund.
