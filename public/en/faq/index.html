Frequently asked questions

What is this?

A New Year project for Armenians wherever they live. For 500 AMD you get your own place on the map of Armenia: one of 111,111 plots of 500×500 m. Every place is a little 3D room that you decorate and can change later, and anyone can step inside and send you warmth. Your place also comes with one wish: you write what you are leaving behind in 2026, in up to 120 characters, and it burns in a shared fire together with thousands of others, on our website and on our YouTube live stream. The rooms and the fire are computer animation and no real paper is burned. What is real is the moment: you, and many others, letting something go together.

How much does it cost?

500 AMD, paid once. For that you get your place on the map with its room and one wish for the fire. The wish is included in the price: there is no extra payment, and papers are no longer sold separately. There are no other options and no hidden charges. From every payment, 100 AMD (20%) goes to New Year gifts for children in orphanages.

When and how do I write my wish?

Right after you buy your place, or later, whenever you like, from the "Fire" tab on the map. Every place has exactly one wish: once it has burned, you can't burn another one for the same place. If you saved your place before card payments opened, you can already write your wish; it goes into the fire as soon as your place is paid.

How fast will my wish burn?

If your place is paid, your wish appears in the fire on the website within minutes of writing it, and you get a page with its number, a replay of it burning and an image to share. From 20 to 31 December wishes also appear on the YouTube live stream, one after another. Your place itself appears on the map right after payment.

Will people see my wish?

Only if you allow it and it passes our check. You can choose to keep your wish private: it still burns, only its number is shown. The fire is public, so we don't show names of real people, phone numbers, links or insults; if your wish contains them, it still burns, but the text is hidden from public view. Your text is always visible to you on your personal page.

How does payment work? Is it safe?

You pay on the bank's own secure payment page, not on our site, and your bank may ask for a confirmation code. We never see or store your card number. Your order is marked as paid only after the bank confirms the payment to us directly.

Which cards can I use, including from abroad?

Visa, Mastercard and ArCa cards accepted by our partner bank; the exact list is shown on the bank's payment page. Most foreign cards work: you are charged in AMD and your bank converts the amount, sometimes with a small fee. Cards issued in Russia generally don't work with Armenian banks; in that case a relative or friend in Armenia can pay for you using the link to your order. Idram may be added later.

Where does the 20% go? Are you a charity?

From every payment we set aside 20% for gifts, and on 31 December we bring New Year gifts to children in orphanages in Armenia. Afterwards we will publish on the site what was bought and where it went. To be honest: we are not a charity but a for-profit company, and you are paying for a digital service. Giving 20% to gifts is our own choice, so it is not a donation from you and it is not tax-deductible.

I'm a blogger. Can I join?

Yes. Fill in the form on the "20% for gifts" page if you would like to come with us on 31 December to deliver the gifts, film it and tell your audience. We'll contact you.

What do I get on the map?

Light up Armenia is a New Year night map of Armenia divided into 111,111 plots of 500×500 m each. This year there are only that many places, and the map won't grow. You choose a free plot, choose how your place looks on the map, write a short message (up to 60 characters) and, if you like, sign it (up to 30 characters). Then you decorate your room: a tree, a sofa, a fireplace, a cat and more. You can change the room later at any time. Visitors can tap your place, step into your room and send you warmth.

Am I buying real land?

No. Your place is a symbolic digital marker and a plot is simply a cell of a picture. You do not acquire any land, property or territory, or any right to a real place. The borders on the map are approximate, drawn from public map data, and say nothing about any legal or political borders.

What if someone else takes my plot?

Each plot can belong to only one person: first come, first served. While you pay, the plot is held for you for 30 minutes. If, rarely, someone else takes it at the same moment, we offer you the nearest free plot or a full refund, whichever you prefer. If you saved your place before payments opened, the plot is not reserved, and if it is taken by then, we offer you the nearest free one.

How long will my place stay on the map?

Your place appears on the map right after payment and stays at least until 31 January 2027. After that we may keep the map as a read-only archive page or remove it. Anyone can tap your place and see your message, your signature (once approved), the date, the province and the nearest town. Your email is never shown.

Can I choose a place in my grandparents' village, or give it as a gift?

Of course. Find the village on the map, choose a free plot and sign it however you like, for example "The Petrosyan family" or "With love, from the grandchildren". You can send the link and image of your place to the person you made it for. Just keep phone numbers, links and other people's private data out of the message, the signature and the wish.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full, if your place didn't appear on the map, your wish didn't burn through our fault, you didn't get your page, you were charged twice or something broke technically. Write to {{company.email}} within 14 days; the money returns to the same card, usually in 5–10 business days.

When is the live stream?

The fire is broadcast live on YouTube from 20 to 31 December 2026. The fire on our website burns every day.

What happens to my data?

We keep only what we need: your wish, your place's message and signature, your room, your email if you left it, and the payment status. We never see your card number, store only a one-way hash of your IP address, don't sell data and don't use ad trackers. You can ask us to delete your text at any time by writing to {{company.email}}.
