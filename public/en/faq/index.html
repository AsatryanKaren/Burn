Frequently asked questions

What is this?

A New Year ritual for Armenians wherever they live. You write what you want to leave behind in 2026, in up to 120 characters, and your paper burns in a shared fire together with thousands of others. The fire is digital: a computer animation on our website and on our YouTube live stream, and no real paper is burned. What is real is the moment: you, and many others, letting something go together. You get a personal page with your paper's number, a replay of it burning and an image to share.

How much does it cost?

500 AMD, whether you burn a paper or light a light on the map. There are no other options and no hidden charges. From every payment, 100 AMD (20%) goes to New Year gifts for children in orphanages.

How fast will my paper burn?

Within minutes of payment your paper appears in the fire on the website, and your personal page opens right away. From 20 to 31 December papers also appear on the YouTube live stream in the order they were paid.

Will people see my text?

Only if you allow it and it passes our check. You can choose to keep your text private: your paper still burns, only its number is shown. The fire is public, so we don't show names of real people, phone numbers, links or insults; if your text contains them, your paper still burns, but the text is hidden from public view. Your text is always visible to you on your personal page.

How does payment work? Is it safe?

You pay on the bank's own secure payment page, not on our site, and your bank may ask for a confirmation code. We never see or store your card number. Your order is marked as paid only after the bank confirms the payment to us directly.

Which cards can I use, including from abroad?

Visa, Mastercard and ArCa cards accepted by our partner bank; the exact list is shown on the bank's payment page. Most foreign cards work: you are charged in AMD and your bank converts the amount, sometimes with a small fee. Cards issued in Russia generally don't work with Armenian banks; in that case a relative or friend in Armenia can pay for you using the link to your order. Idram may be added later.

Where does the 20% go? Are you a charity?

From every payment we set aside 20% for gifts, and on 31 December we bring New Year gifts to children in orphanages in Armenia. Afterwards we will publish on the site what was bought and where it went. To be honest: we are not a charity but a for-profit company, and you are paying for a digital service. Giving 20% to gifts is our own choice, so it is not a donation from you and it is not tax-deductible.

I'm a blogger. Can I join?

Yes. Fill in the form on the "20% for gifts" page if you would like to come with us on 31 December to deliver the gifts, film it and tell your audience. We'll contact you.

What is Light up Armenia?

A New Year night map of Armenia divided into 7,048 plots of 2×2 km each. You choose a free plot and place your light on it: a tree, a glowing home, a candle, a star, a gift or a pomegranate. You pick a colour, write your message (up to 60 characters) and, if you like, sign it (up to 30 characters). A light costs 500 AMD, and just like with papers, 20% of the payment goes to New Year gifts for children in orphanages.

Am I buying real land?

No. Your light is a symbolic digital marker and a plot is simply a cell of a picture. You do not acquire any land, property or territory, or any right to a real place. The borders on the map are approximate, drawn from public map data, and say nothing about any legal or political borders.

What if someone else takes my plot?

There is only one light per plot: first come, first served. While you pay, the plot is held for you for 30 minutes. If, rarely, someone else takes it at the same moment, we offer you the nearest free plot or a full refund, whichever you prefer. If you saved your light before payments opened, the plot is not reserved, and if it is taken by then, we offer you the nearest free one.

How long will my light stay on?

Your light appears on the map right after payment and stays at least until 31 January 2027. After that we may keep the map as a read-only archive page or remove it. Anyone can tap your light and see your message, your signature (once approved), the date, the province and the nearest town. Your email is never shown.

Can I light one in my grandparents' village, or as a gift?

Of course. Find the village on the map, choose a free plot and sign it however you like, for example "The Petrosyan family" or "With love, from the grandchildren". You can send the link and image of your light to the person you lit it for. Just keep phone numbers, links and other people's private data out of the message and signature.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full, if your paper didn't burn or your light didn't appear on the map, you didn't get your page, you were charged twice or something broke technically. Write to {{company.email}} within 14 days; the money returns to the same card, usually in 5–10 business days.

When is the live stream?

The fire is broadcast live on YouTube from 20 to 31 December 2026. The fire on our website burns every day.

What happens to my data?

We keep only what we need: your text or your light's message, your email if you left it, and the payment status. We never see your card number, store only a one-way hash of your IP address, don't sell data and don't use ad trackers. You can ask us to delete your text at any time by writing to {{company.email}}.
