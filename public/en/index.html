Questions

What is this?

A New Year ritual for Armenians wherever they live. You write what you want to leave behind in the old year, in up to 120 characters, and your paper burns in a shared fire together with thousands of others. You get a personal page with your paper's number, a replay of it burning and an image to share.

Is it real fire?

No, the fire is digital: a computer animation on our website and on our YouTube live stream. No real paper is burned and nothing is sent to you by post. What is real is the moment: you, and many others, letting something go together.

How fast will my paper burn?

Within minutes of payment your paper appears in the fire on the website, and your personal page opens right away. From 20 to 31 December papers also appear on the live stream in the order they were paid. Midnight fire papers wait for the final minutes of 31 December.

Will people see my text?

Only if you allow it and it passes our check. You can choose to keep your text private: your paper still burns, only its number is shown. Your text is always visible to you on your personal page.

Why can't I write names?

The fire is public, and the people you name never agreed to appear on a live stream. So we don't show names of real people, phone numbers, links or insults. If your text contains them, your paper still burns, but the text is hidden from public view.

How does payment work? Is it safe?

You pay on the bank's own secure payment page, not on our site, and your bank may ask for a confirmation code. We never see or store your card number. Your order is marked as paid only after the bank confirms the payment to us directly.
